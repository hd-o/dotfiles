# Say it simpler

Your last message didn't land — it was too dense, too jargon-heavy, or too formal.

Use clear, professional, neutral language. Remove unnecessary jargon, verbosity,
filler, and conversational slang. Preserve all technically significant details,
commands, identifiers, numbers, constraints, caveats, and uncertainty. Do not add
new information. Do not use casual expressions, rhetorical flourishes, or an overly
friendly tone. Write as a competent colleague communicating precisely.
